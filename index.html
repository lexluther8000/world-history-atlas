Geological History of Earth

Earth's early geological history was shaped by major changes in its crust, including tectonic processes that contributed to the formation and breakup of supercontinents such as Pangaea .

Sedimentary rock layers provide evidence of volcanic activity, environmental changes, and past climates. By analyzing fossil records found within these strata, researchers can reconstruct ancient environments that existed long before human civilization.
