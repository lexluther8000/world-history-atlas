Geological History of Earth

The early geological formation of the planet involved massive shifting of the crust, similar to the tectonic changes seen during the Pangea era, before cooling down completely. Scientists study these plate boundaries to understand how continents drifted apart over hundreds of millions of years.

Sedimentary rock layers provide a clear timeline of volcanic activity and climate shifts. By analyzing fossil records found within these deep strata, researchers can reconstruct the ancient environments that existed long before human civilization.
